# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 14 (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS, specified by the user.
Framer Motion for all motion. No deploy target specified.

## Users

Owners and custodians of exotic, collector, and historically significant cars —
the person responsible for a car that is an appreciating asset rather than
transport. They arrive already knowing what detailing is; they are evaluating
whether this shop can be trusted with a car whose paint may be irreplaceable.

Secondary: collection managers, family offices, and marque specialists who
place cars on an owner's behalf and need to judge process and liability
before recommending a shop.

The visit is deliberate, usually on desktop, often before a season, a sale,
or a show entry. The job is not "book a wash" — it is "decide whether to
begin a conversation."

## Product Purpose

Meridian Concours is an appointment-only care practice for exotic and
collector cars: paint correction and preservation, concours preparation,
and ongoing custodianship for cars in storage or active collections.

The site exists to convert deliberation into an enquiry. Success is a
qualified assessment request, not volume.

## Positioning

Custodianship, not car washing. The practice is organised around preserving
originality and provenance — the deliberate choice to remove less material,
document what was done, and treat a car's existing finish as historical
evidence. A volume detailer competing on turnaround cannot truthfully make
that claim, because it is a constraint on throughput, not a service tier.

## Operating Context

- Engagements begin with an in-person or photographic assessment, not a price list.
- Work is measured in days and weeks, not hours; cars are often retained on site.
- Owners frequently arrive via a marque specialist, club, or concours entry deadline.
- Documentation (paint depth readings, condition records, before/after) is part
  of the deliverable and travels with the car.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Service tiers: Custodianship (ongoing), Concours Preparation (event-driven),
  Collection Care (multi-car, storage-integrated).
- Pricing is not published. Tiers carry scope, duration, and cadence; the
  commercial answer is "on application" behind an assessment request.
- Primary conversion action: request an assessment. No online booking, no
  instant quote, no e-commerce.
- Undecided: real service geography, legal entity, contact details, insurance
  and liability wording. Placeholders must remain visibly placeholder.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Meridian Concours. Register: brand — this is a marketing surface where
design is the product.

Voice: confident and quiet. Understatement over superlative. Never "ultimate",
"premium", "luxury", "unrivalled". The work is described in specifics
(process, materials, time) rather than adjectives.

Anti-references the user made binding by choosing this positioning: this must
not read as a volume car-wash or a startup landing page.

## Evidence on Hand

None real. This is a demonstration build.

- Social proof uses clearly-fictional owner clubs and concours events, with
  quotes attributed to invented owners by initials and car. No real brands,
  no real people, no real press.
- No photography has been supplied. Any imagery is placeholder and must be
  replaced before this is used commercially.
- No pricing, testimonials, certifications, insurance terms, or client names
  may be presented as fact.

## Product Principles

1. **Restraint is the proof.** The visitor is judging judgement. Anything that
   over-sells contradicts the positioning.
2. **Specifics over adjectives.** Name the process, the material, the duration.
   Never claim quality — demonstrate the standard of thinking.
3. **The car leads.** Imagery and artefacts outrank interface. The page should
   feel like it is deferring to the object.
4. **Enquiry, not transaction.** Every path ends at an assessment request.
   Nothing simulates instant commerce.
5. **Placeholders stay legible as placeholders.** In a demo, invented content
   must never harden into apparent fact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 body contrast, visible keyboard focus on every
interactive element, and `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured on all motion.
Audience skews older than a typical tech-product audience — body type must
not go below 16px and long-form copy needs generous measure and leading.
